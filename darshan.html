Daily rhythm

When the temple is open

Morning session 4:00 AM to 12:00 noon. Evening session 4:00 PM to 8:00 PM. Guruthy follows the night closing. Festival days and special nivedyam can shift the clock — ask at the counter when you arrive.

Tuesdays, Fridays, and the Sabarimala season are the busiest. A head bath before darshan is the usual custom.

Vazhipadu

Offerings

Nakshatra Pushpanjali

Flower offering in the name of a birth star. One of the two vazhipadu highlighted on the present official site.

Guruthy

The night offering associated with Bhadrakali. Performed after the nada closes. Book ahead on crowded days.

Archana & Rektha Pushpanjali

Name-and-star archana, and the red-flower offering used in this Shakta house.

Turmeric, pepper, anklet

Common festival offerings include turmeric, pepper, coconut, and — remembering Kannagi — an anklet. Vysoori is offered turmeric and rice bran.

Before you enter

Dress and custom

Dress

Men: mundu or veshti. Women: sari, set mundu, or salwar kameez with dupatta. This is the custom reported by devotees; the counter will guide you if you are unsure.

Photography

No photographs inside the temple. The images on this site are of the outer courtyard, festivals in the open, or paintings of legend — never a camera in the sanctum.

The day

At different hours she is worshipped as Saraswati, Lakshmi, and Durga-Kannagi. If you can, stay for Deeparadhana. Do not treat Bharani as a spectacle; it is worship, even when it is loud.
